● Compare how POST behaves differently from GET. 



Post involves interacting with the server, and providing an input. As a result, there is a larger raw content length (380 bytes). The remainder is the same as a get request. 


● Explain the purpose of the payload (key=value) and how it’s transmitted. 

The purpose is to get input from the client. It is transmitted through the POST method.

Because it doesn’t exist


403 (Forbidden): What does this tell you about access restrictions? 
It returned this error because it is an http request. There might be additional permissions required, the IP might be blocked, a VPN could be active or old cookies
